# Backlog (not implemented)

These ideas are intentionally deferred from the disposable MVP:

- Batch check for up to 20 images and an exportable report.
- Approved Photoroom affiliate URL after program approval.
- A privacy-reviewed production analytics endpoint.
- A versioned rule update process when Amazon seller guidance changes.
- Optional downloadable local checklist.
- Category-specific guidance only after separate evidence and copy review.

Explicitly out of scope: Etsy, eBay, Shopify, TikTok Shop, Walmart, Seller Central API, SP-API, OAuth, accounts, database, subscriptions, AI chat, image generation, background removal, and a large backend.
